# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers first; investors, startup contacts, and event organizers second. They arrive from a CV link, LinkedIn, or a conference badge scan (often on a phone) and skim for signal: credentials, shipped products, awards, and a way to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Luka Đelošević: MSc ECE student, co-founder & CPO of Sokolus, co-founder of BLDxp (TripVice), InnovNation Serbia 2025 winner, Web Summit Qatar 2026 speaker. Success means a recruiter or partner reaches the contact section convinced of technical skill and initiative, then reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Technical, playful, confident. A dark hacker-terminal world (boot sequence, interactive mini-shell, Konami egg, confetti) executed with precision. The playfulness proves the skill; it never undermines it.

## Anti-references

- Generic template portfolios: centered hero, icon-card grids, purple gradients.
- Corporate CV sites: sterile white, stock photography, buzzword copy.
- No cyan, no Outfit font, no light theme. The lime-on-near-black terminal identity is fixed (the accent stays user-tweakable via the Tweaks panel).

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the proof.** Every interaction (shell, boot, tilt) demonstrates engineering craft; anything broken or janky contradicts the pitch.
2. **Recruiter-first reading path.** Credentials, projects, and contact must be reachable and legible in a 60-second phone skim.
3. **Playful, never gimmicky.** Easter eggs are opt-in and skippable; core content never depends on them.
4. **One aesthetic, tweakable surface.** Dark terminal plus lime accent is the identity; user tweaks (accent, font, motion) personalize it without breaking it.
5. **Motion respects the visitor.** Reduced-motion support, calm mode, and tab-hidden pausing are non-negotiable, on every new feature too.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` fully (already implemented: CSS kill-switch, boot skip, pause-on-hide). Keep parity for anything new.
- Touch parity: everything reachable by keyboard must be reachable by touch (boot skip, section navigation).
- Sensible WCAG AA targets: body text contrast, 44px touch targets, visible focus.
